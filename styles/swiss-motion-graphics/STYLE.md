# Style: `swiss-motion-graphics` (Swiss International Grid)

## Essence
Inspired by Josef Müller-Brockmann poster design: stark asymmetric modular grids, high-contrast grotesque typography, matte bone-white canvas, deep ink black, and one bold signal color (`#FF2A00`).

## Visual Palette
- **Background**: Warm Paper White `#F4F1EA`
- **Primary Ink**: Stark Black `#111111`
- **Signal Accent**: Swiss Vermilion Red `#FF2A00`
- **Typography**: `'Helvetica Neue', 'Inter', 'Arial Black', sans-serif`, tight line-height, heavy weights (`800`–`900`).

## Motion Grammar
- Everything snaps along visible 12-column grid hairlines.
- Geometric primitives (circles, bars, squares) animate with sharp `Easing.outQuint` curves.

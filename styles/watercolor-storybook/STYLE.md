# Style: `watercolor-storybook` (Organic Watercolor & Paper Grain)

## Essence
Hand-painted storybook aesthetic built with translucent overlapping polygon washes, warm cotton paper background (`#FBF7EE`), and gentle camera drift.

## Visual Palette
- **Paper Base**: Warm Cream `#FBF7EE`
- **Pigments**: Indigo Wash `rgba(43, 76, 126, 0.38)`, Terracotta `rgba(217, 107, 67, 0.42)`, Sage `rgba(92, 138, 108, 0.38)`
- **Ink Line**: Warm Sepia `#3A2E28`
- **Typography**: Serif (`'Georgia', 'Times New Roman', serif`), italic narrative captions.
